# Salary Research and Negotiation

Salary transparency laws now cover CA, CO, NY, WA, IL, and other states/cities — postings in
those areas must include ranges. Use data before any negotiation, in any field.

KEY DATA SOURCES (free):
- Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) OES: government wage benchmarks by occupation and metro —
  the most comprehensive cross-industry source (nursing, trades, finance, education, etc.).
- Industry/role salary guides (e.g., Robert Half for finance/admin, Glassdoor, Levels.fyi for
  tech) — directional; cross-check against BLS.
- Professional associations and unions often publish wage scales for licensed fields.
- Posted ranges in transparency-law states for the same title/level.

NEGOTIATION TIPS:
- Never give the first number. If pressed, give a range with your target near the bottom.
- Compare total compensation (base + bonus + differentials/overtime + benefits), not just base.
- Research the band before the offer call and cite your source confidently.
- Counter every offer exactly once, in writing, with a specific number.
- For licensed/shift roles, factor shift differentials, on-call pay, and certification premiums.
