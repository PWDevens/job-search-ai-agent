# ATS Red Flags: What Gets Resumes Automatically Rejected

Common automatic rejection triggers in ATS and LLM screeners (all fields):

FORMATTING RED FLAGS:
- Tables, text boxes, headers/footers (content invisible to the parser)
- Graphics, icons, infographic-style resumes
- Two-column layouts (parser reads columns as one jumbled stream)
- Non-standard section headers
- Missing dates on jobs

CONTENT RED FLAGS:
- Job-hopping pattern: 3+ jobs in under 18 months each, with no explanation
- Overqualification mismatch: senior title applying for a junior role with no context
- Buzzword stuffing with no supporting evidence ("synergy," "passionate")
- Generic objective statement ("Seeking a challenging role...")
- Unexplained employment gaps (a one-line note resolves most concerns)

TECHNICAL RED FLAGS:
- PDF created from a scan or photo (no selectable text)
- File name with spaces/special chars: "My Resume FINAL v3 (1).pdf" → use "FirstLast_Resume_2026.pdf"
- File size over 5 MB

MITIGATION:
- Test your resume through a free ATS checker (e.g., Jobscan, Resume Worded free tier)
- Submit as DOCX unless PDF is specifically requested
- Use a resume-specific filename with your name and year
