# AI-Driven Hiring: LLM Screening and What It Means for Candidates

A growing share of enterprise hiring funnels use LLM-based resume screening (e.g., HireVue,
Eightfold, Phenom). These differ from traditional ATS:

HOW LLM SCREENERS WORK:
- They read the whole resume as a document, not as parsed fields.
- They compare it semantically against the job description — exact keywords matter less;
  contextual relevance matters more.
- They score candidates on dimensions: skills match, career trajectory, tenure stability,
  and role relevance.
- They may generate a recruiter summary (e.g., "8 years of ICU nursing with strong patient
  outcomes but limited charge-nurse experience").

IMPLICATIONS FOR CANDIDATES (any field):
1. Write in clear, natural prose — not disconnected fragments. LLMs parse full sentences better.
2. Demonstrate impact in context: "Cut month-end close from 10 to 4 days by automating
   reconciliations" beats "Improved closing process."
3. Use a brief professional summary (3–5 sentences) at the top — screeners weight it heavily.
4. Quantify everything you can (patients/shift, revenue, projects delivered, students taught).
5. Show progression. Random lateral moves without narrative context score lower; add brief
   context for contract roles or career pivots.
