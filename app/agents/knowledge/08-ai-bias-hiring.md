# Understanding AI Bias in Hiring and How to Navigate It

AI hiring tools have documented biases candidates can partially mitigate (applies across fields):

KNOWN BIAS VECTORS:
- Name-based bias: Resumes with non-Anglo names receive fewer callbacks in studies
  (Kline et al., 2022). Some candidates use initials for initial screening.
- Photo bias: Never include a photo on US/UK resumes (illegal to request; biases screeners).
- Address bias: Some ATS filter by zip code. If open to relocation, use "City, State Area" or
  "Open to Relocation" instead of a full address.
- Education/prestige bias: Counter by emphasizing skills, licenses, projects, and outcomes.
- Tenure bias: Screeners may penalize frequent job changes even when justified. Add "(Contract)"
  after non-permanent roles.

PROACTIVE STRATEGIES:
- Favor companies with blind/anonymized first-round processes.
- Apply via employee referral when possible (often bypasses ATS).
- Target employers explicitly practicing skills-based hiring.
- Disclose gaps proactively with a one-line explanation in the summary.
