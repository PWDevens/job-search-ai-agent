# Keyword Optimization Strategies

The single most effective ATS tactic is keyword mirroring: use the exact language from
the job description in your resume. This works in any field.

STRATEGY 1 — TITLE MIRRORING:
If the posting says "Charge Nurse" or "Senior Accountant," your most recent title line
should include that term even if your official title differed. Add it in parentheses:
"Staff Nurse III (Charge Nurse)."

STRATEGY 2 — SKILLS MATRIX:
Create a skills section listing the tools, methods, certifications, and competencies the
role calls for. Update it per application to mirror the posting's priorities.

STRATEGY 3 — CONTEXT KEYWORDS:
Beyond hard skills, ATS and reviewers score action and impact language: "led," "managed,"
"reduced," "increased," "supervised." Use verbs that match the seniority of the target role.

STRATEGY 4 — EDUCATION & CREDENTIALS:
Spell out degrees, licenses, and certifications fully with the issuer:
"Bachelor of Science in Nursing"; "Professional Engineer (PE), State of Texas, 2023";
"CPA (AICPA, 2022)." Abbreviations alone are often missed by parsers.

STRATEGY 5 — AVOID OVER-STUFFING:
Keyword stuffing (hidden white text, font-color tricks) is instantly detected by modern
ATS and results in automatic disqualification.
