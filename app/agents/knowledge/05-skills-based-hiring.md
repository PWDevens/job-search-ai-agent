# Skills-Based Hiring: The Shift Away from Credentials

Since 2022, skills-based hiring has accelerated. Many large employers now state they do not
require a 4-year degree for a range of roles, weighting demonstrated skills instead.

WHAT THIS MEANS:
- Certifications, licenses, apprenticeships, portfolios, and hands-on assessments carry real
  weight when presented clearly.
- Recruiters increasingly filter on verified skills and assessment badges.
- Demonstrable proof of competence (a portfolio, a license number, a measurable result) beats
  a generic claim.

HOW TO LEVERAGE IT (by field):
1. Earn and list relevant credentials: licenses (RN, PE, CPA, journeyman/master electrician),
   micro-credentials (Coursera, edX, DataCamp), and trade certifications.
2. Show proof of work: a portfolio (design, writing), a project list (engineering, ops),
   measurable outcomes (sales numbers, patient/throughput metrics).
3. Add verified skill badges where the platform offers them (e.g., LinkedIn Skills Assessments).
4. Quantify time-to-competency for new skills ("Earned forklift + OSHA 30 certification within
   first month").
