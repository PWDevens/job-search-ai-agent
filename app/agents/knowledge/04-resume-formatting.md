# Resume Formatting Best Practices for Human Readers

Once through ATS, your resume reaches a human reviewer who spends 6–10 seconds on first scan.

VISUAL HIERARCHY:
- Name in large bold at top (18–22pt).
- Contact info on one line: City, State | email | phone | (LinkedIn/portfolio if relevant).
- Section headers left-aligned, bold, with a horizontal rule.
- Consistent font (Calibri, Garamond, or Arial) at 10–11pt for body.

ACHIEVEMENT FORMAT — PROBLEM → ACTION → RESULT:
Bad:  "Responsible for patient care."
Good: "Managed a 6-patient ICU assignment per shift, maintaining a zero-fall record over
       18 months."
Bad:  "Handled accounts."
Good: "Owned AP for 200+ vendors, cutting late-payment penalties 30% in one year."

LENGTH RULES:
- Under 5 years experience: 1 page.
- 5–15 years: 1–2 pages.
- 15+ years / executive / academic: 2–3 pages.
- Never truncate real experience just to fit one page.

SECTIONS (typical order):
1. Contact / Header
2. Professional Summary (3–5 sentences; optional for experienced candidates)
3. Skills / Core Competencies
4. Work Experience (reverse chronological)
5. Education
6. Licenses / Certifications (critical in healthcare, trades, finance, engineering)
7. Projects / Portfolio / Publications (where relevant to the field)
