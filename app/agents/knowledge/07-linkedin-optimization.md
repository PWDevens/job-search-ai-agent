# LinkedIn Profile Optimization for Job Search

LinkedIn's recruiter search ranks profiles by keyword relevance, profile completeness,
recency of activity, connections at the hiring company, and skills. This helps candidates
in every field that recruiters source on the platform.

HIGH-IMPACT OPTIMIZATIONS:
1. HEADLINE (220 chars): Don't just paste your title. Lead with role + key strengths, e.g.,
   "Registered Nurse | ICU & Critical Care | BLS/ACLS" or
   "Senior Accountant | GAAP · Month-End Close · SOX."
2. ABOUT SECTION (2,600 chars): First person, mirror keywords from target roles, end with a
   clear call to action ("Open to charge-nurse roles in the Southeast").
3. FEATURED SECTION: Pin proof of work — a portfolio, certification, project, or recommendation.
4. EXPERIENCE: Match your resume; 3–5 achievement-focused bullets per role.
5. SKILLS: Add up to 50, prioritizing those in your target postings; gather endorsements.
6. OPEN TO WORK: Use the badge (optionally hidden from your employer). Set target titles,
   locations, and start date.
7. ACTIVITY: Engage a few times a week in your industry; active profiles rank higher.
